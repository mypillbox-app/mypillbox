MyPillBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Your information stays on your device. MyPillBox does not collect your data, and it does not send your data anywhere.

Who we are

MyPillBox: Medication Reminder ("MyPillBox", "the app") is made by Michael Dominguez. You can reach us at MyPillBox@yahoo.com.

What the app stores

The medications you enter (names, dosages, notes, doctors, times, and amounts on hand), your record of which doses you took or skipped, and your settings. All of it is saved only inside the app on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive it.

What we do not do

MyPillBox does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you. It has no account or sign-in. It has no servers, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party tools. It does not need an internet connection to work, and none of your medication information is stored on any server.

Notifications

Dose reminders are scheduled on your own device and are never sent from a server. If you wear an Apple Watch, iOS shows the same reminders there. The number on the app icon is worked out on your device from your medication list. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device's Settings at any time.

Sharing your medication list

You can print, email, or copy your medication list from the app's Settings. This happens only when you choose it. The list goes where you send it, such as your mail app, a printer, or another app. Once it leaves MyPillBox, the privacy policy of that app or service applies.

Backups

If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, the backup may include the app's data. That is handled by Apple under your own Apple Account and settings. MyPillBox itself never sends anything to iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Permissions

The app asks only for permission to send notifications, so it can remind you to take your medication. It never asks for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.

Children

MyPillBox collects no information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

MyPillBox is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email MyPillBox@yahoo.com.
