Common questions

I'm not getting reminders.

Check three things. In MyPillBox, open Settings and make sure Medication Reminders is on. On your iPhone, open Settings, Notifications, MyPillBox, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Finally, make sure the medication has a time set and hasn't been paused.

Why do reminders appear on my Apple Watch?

iOS shows an app's notifications on your Apple Watch when your iPhone is locked. To stop this, open the Watch app on your iPhone, tap Notifications, find MyPillBox, and turn off Allow Notifications.

What does the number on the app icon mean?

It is how many of today's doses are due now or within the next hour and haven't been taken.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your device. MyPillBox has no account and no servers, so we never see your medications. Read the full privacy policy.

Can I move my medications to a new phone?

MyPillBox does not sync between devices. If you restore a new iPhone from a backup of your old one, your MyPillBox data comes with it. You can also share your medication list from Settings by print, email, or copy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your device. That removes all of its data. You can also delete a single medication from its edit page.
