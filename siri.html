Setting up Siri with MyPillBox

Ask Siri what is next, how many are left, and tell it you took your pills.

1. What to say

Say "Hey Siri" (or hold the side button), then one of these. Always include the name MyPillBox:

"Which pills are next in MyPillBox?"
"How many pills are left in MyPillBox?"
"I took my pills in MyPillBox."

The first time, Siri may ask whether to turn on shortcuts for MyPillBox. Say or tap yes.

2. Marking pills taken

When you say "I took my pills in MyPillBox", Siri names the medicines due and asks you to confirm. Say Yes (or tap Yes) and it marks them as taken. Say No to cancel. Nothing is marked until you confirm.

3. Make Siri say the answers out loud

If Siri only shows a message on the screen and doesn't speak it, change this setting on your iPhone:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone (the gray gear).
  2. Tap Siri. On some iPhones this is called Apple Intelligence & Siri or Siri & Search.
  3. Find Spoken Responses (it may be inside Siri Responses) and tap it.
  4. Choose Prefer Spoken Responses.

With Automatic, Siri speaks when you can't look at the screen (for example with headphones or in the car) and often only shows the answer when you are looking at it.

4. Spoken reminders (optional)

MyPillBox can also say "Time to take your medication" out loud when a reminder arrives.

  1. Open MyPillBox and tap Settings.
  2. Switch on Speak reminders aloud. Tap Hear a sample to try it.

Nothing is spoken when your iPhone is on silent, and the volume follows your ringer volume. If you wear an Apple Watch, iOS sends reminders to the watch while your iPhone is locked, so the voice plays only when the iPhone itself receives the reminder. The watch uses its own bell and a tap on your wrist.

If it doesn't work

  • Say the phrase exactly, including MyPillBox. Phrases with "medication" or "dose" may go to Apple's Health app instead.
  • Make sure Siri is turned on in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Open the iPhone's Settings, scroll down to MyPillBox, and make sure its Siri options are on.
  • You can also find these actions in the Shortcuts app, under MyPillBox. Tap one to run it without speaking.
  • Still stuck? Email MyPillBox@yahoo.com.

MyPillBox is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.
