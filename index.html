Common questions

I'm not getting reminders.

Check three things. In MyPillBox, open Settings and make sure Medication Reminders is on. On your iPhone, open Settings, Notifications, MyPillBox, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Finally, make sure the medication has a time set and hasn't been paused.

How do I turn on spoken reminders?

Open Settings in MyPillBox and switch on Speak reminders aloud. When a reminder arrives, your iPhone says "Time to take your medication." Tap Hear a sample to try it. Nothing is spoken when your iPhone is on silent, and the volume follows your ringer volume. If you wear an Apple Watch, iOS sends reminders to the watch instead while your iPhone is locked, so the voice plays only when the iPhone itself receives the reminder.

How do I make the text bigger?

Open Settings in MyPillBox and choose Text size: Standard, Large, or Extra Large. MyPillBox also follows your iPhone's own text size, so if you already use larger text in your iPhone's Display & Brightness settings, the app will follow it.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

Install MyPillBox on your iPhone, then open the Watch app on your iPhone and make sure MyPillBox is set to show on your Apple Watch. On your watch, MyPillBox shows today's doses grouped by time. Tap a time to mark everything due then as taken. The change appears on your iPhone right away. Your watch needs to be paired with the iPhone that has your medications on it. If the list looks empty, open MyPillBox on your iPhone once.

What do the colors on my watch face mean?

MyPillBox has a watch face complication. Blue background means your next dose is more than an hour away. Green background means it is coming up within the hour. Amber background means it is past due. A check mark means everything is taken. Some watch faces show everything in one color; on those, MyPillBox uses a pill symbol and a bell instead of colors. If colors are hard for you to tell apart, open Settings in MyPillBox and, under Appearance, choose Monochrome for Colors. The whole app turns to shades of gray, and the watch uses shapes instead of colors.

Why is my watch face slow to change after I mark a dose taken?

The icon on your watch face can take a few minutes to catch up after you mark a dose taken. Your watch decides when to refresh its face. Opening the MyPillBox app on your watch updates it right away.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the plus button, search for MyPillBox, and choose Small, Medium, or Large. Small and Medium show your next dose. Large lists every dose you still have to take. A Lock Screen version is also available.

How do I use Siri?

Step-by-step help with pictures: Setting up Siri

Try saying "Which pills are next in MyPillBox", "How many pills are left in MyPillBox", or "I took my pills in MyPillBox". Siri asks you to confirm before it marks anything as taken. You can also find these actions in the Shortcuts app. If Siri answers with something else, use the exact words above including "MyPillBox". If Siri only shows its answer on screen and doesn't speak it, open your iPhone's Settings, tap Siri, find Spoken Responses, and choose Prefer Spoken Responses.

Why do reminders appear on my Apple Watch?

iOS shows an app's notifications on your Apple Watch when your iPhone is locked. To stop this, open the Watch app on your iPhone, tap Notifications, find MyPillBox, and turn off Allow Notifications.

What does the number on the app icon mean?

It is how many of today's doses are due now or within the next hour and haven't been taken.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your own devices. MyPillBox has no account and no servers, so we never see your medications. Your iPhone shares your dose list with your Apple Watch and with the widget directly on your devices. Read the full privacy policy.

Can I move my medications to a new phone?

MyPillBox does not sync between devices, but you can back up and restore. On your old iPhone, open Settings in MyPillBox, tap Back up, and save the file in the Files app (for example in iCloud Drive). On your new iPhone, install MyPillBox, open Settings, tap Restore, and choose that file. If you restore a new iPhone from a backup of your old one, your MyPillBox data also comes with it.

How do I back up and restore my information?

Open Settings in MyPillBox and find Back up and restore. Back up saves your medications, history and settings to a file, in the Files app on your iPhone or in iCloud Drive. Each backup on the same day gets a number at the end of its name. Restore reads a backup file and replaces everything in the app with it, after asking you to confirm.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your device. That removes all of its data. You can also delete a single medication from its edit page.
